Matte near-black graphite surfaces, a subtle mineral grain, aged-silver details and very restrained warm antique gold. Warm ivory for primary text, quiet warm grey secondary text, a soft desaturated jade only for on states. Refined Chinese serif titles, clean highly readable Chinese sans-serif labels. Crisp purposeful edges with very small chamfers. Beautiful custom engraved compass, crossed-swords and stag/hunting insignia in a unified relief style. Fine believable metallic highlights, no bright bloom. Premium, calm, tactile and specifically made for this fantasy game. NO leather slab, NO brown rectangles, NO SaaS/admin cards, NO web-app drawer aesthetics, NO oversized gold filigree around everything, NO purple glass, NO garish mobile MMO chrome.
